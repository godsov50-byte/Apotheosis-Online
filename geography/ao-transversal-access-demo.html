Shared model rules
  • Spatial entities link through access points—not direct scripted jumps.
  • Each system has its own traversal profile and constraints.
  • Water propagation follows elevation/flow connectivity, not creature passability.
  • Creatures and bandits can share nodes but not necessarily the same edges.
  • Access points may be blocked, gated, damaged, flooded, or conditionally usable.
