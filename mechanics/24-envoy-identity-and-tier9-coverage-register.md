# AO Chess — Envoy Identity and Tier 9 Coverage Register

**Status: PROPOSED / WORKING REGISTER / NOT CANON.** This register consolidates the previously discussed Envoy lineage hierarchy, working origin-lineage roster, being-type classifications, scale dimension, and the Chessboard elemental-axis proposal into testable Tier 9 coverage slots. It does not approve new lore, merge lineages without evidence, or resolve creator-deferred affinity records.

## 1. Purpose and relationship to the benchmark framework

This register is the source inventory companion to [23 — Tier 9 Envoy and Counter-Creature Benchmark Framework](23-tier-9-envoy-creature-benchmark-framework.md).

- Document 23 defines benchmark records, paired counter-creatures, coverage rules, and combat tests.
- This document lists the working Envoy identity values and coverage slots that document 23 must exercise.
- The existing ten creature racial types remain a separate creature taxonomy in document 22.
- The existing ten Envoy classes and ten creature class-counter archetypes remain in document 21.
- The existing Chessboard elemental axes remain a proposal in document 20.
- The exact affinity/weakness records in document 03 remain creator-deferred UNKNOWN. Document 20 does not overwrite them.

The accepted identity hierarchy is:

**Race → Ancestry → Sub-ancestry**

A sub-ancestry may be absent. Race/ancestry are identity lineage classifications, not Envoy class, being type, subtype, scale, affinity, or creature archetype.

- Keep half-lineages and mixed descent as explicit ancestry/heritage relationships rather than treating every hybrid label as a separate biological origin.

### 3.3 Scale values

The following scale dimension was previously outlined:

1. Individual
2. Group
3. Army
4. Structure
5. City
6. Country
7. World

Scale is not Tier, class, size, rank, rarity, or combat archetype. It identifies the scope/scale at which an entity or profile operates. A character benchmark normally uses Individual unless the entity's actual identity and mechanics justify another scale. Do not assign a city/world scale merely because an Envoy has a large-area technique.

### 3.4 Archetype remains a separate axis

Prior archetype examples include Anti-Unit, Anti-Army, Anti-Fortress, Anti-City, Anti-Country, Anti-World, Anti-Divine, Anti-Dragon, Anti-Thaumaturgy, and Anti-Spirit. These examples are not a replacement for the ten Envoy classes and are not the same field as being type or scale. Preserve the distinction:

- Class: combat role/class.
- Archetype: specialized combat function or target profile.
- Being type: nature/kind of being.
- Subtype: more specific being classification.
- Scale: operational scope.
- Tier: power/evolution tier, with Tier 0 highest and Tier 9 lowest.

## 4. Affinity coverage inventory

### 4.1 Creator-deferred affinity source

The finalized complete affinity roster and directed weakness/resistance relationships in mechanics document 03 are source-access UNKNOWN and creator-deferred. This is an explicit unresolved source state, not evidence that the roster does not exist. Do not invent a canonical affinity graph, fill missing edges as neutral, or infer weakness from elemental intuition.

### 4.2 Chessboard elemental-axis proposal

Mechanics document 20 contains a separate working Chessboard proposal:

**Eight-element cycle:** Fire, Wood, Earth, Lightning, Water, Ice, Wind, Metal.

**Separate special axis:** Light and Shadow.

The proposed circular order is Fire → Wood → Earth → Lightning → Water → Ice → Wind → Metal → Fire. The proposed two-advantage/two-disadvantage chart is still a draft for review, not a recovered canonical chart. Light/Shadow interactions with one another and with the eight elements remain context- or technique-dependent until defined.

For benchmark bookkeeping, create provisional individual coverage slots for all ten named proposal values, but mark them proposal_blocked until their canonical status, actual affinity ownership/access, and relevant weakness/defense rules are confirmed. Do not let a benchmark slot imply that the Envoy is granted that affinity.

### 4.3 Composite / derived affinity

Composite or derived expressions are acknowledged, but no complete roster, recipe, eligibility rule, or matchup graph is established here. Mud appears in the affinity source as an illustrative derived expression, not proof of a new foundational affinity. Record composites only when a sourced recipe or explicit creator decision exists. Do not manufacture every possible pairwise combination.

### 4.4 Affinity record distinctions

For each Envoy, preserve separate records for:
- acquired/owned affinity;
- currently accessible affinity;
- active drawing/attunement in the test;
- EA reserve for each accessible affinity;
- acquired techniques and their affinity requirements;
- proficiency/mastery/progression;
- charge or buildup, if separately defined;
- owned-affinity weakness facts;
- action-specific active defense effects.

Ownership does not imply access, active drawing, technique knowledge, or simultaneous use. AP and EA remain independent.

## 6. Combination coverage

The complete benchmark set must test:

- each class with each valid race/ancestry lineage;
- each class with each valid being type/subtype;
- each class with each valid foundational affinity;
- each class with each valid composite affinity;
- compatible race + ancestry + sub-ancestry + being type + class + affinity combinations;
- relevant scale and archetype interactions where they are mechanically meaningful.

Do not blindly generate every mathematical permutation. Use the document 23 compatibility states: valid, invalid, conditional, and unknown. Each valid combination receives an explicit combination ID and its own test evidence. Each invalid or conditional combination records the rule/source that establishes its status. Unknown combinations remain blocked.

If the number of valid combinations becomes large, use a coverage matrix and pairwise/higher-order test strategy to prioritize execution, but do not claim full exhaustive combination coverage until every valid combination has an explicit coverage record.

## 7. Counter-creature pairing rules

Each Envoy benchmark slot must have a paired Tier 9 creature designed against the actual profile being tested.

- The ten class slots reuse the ten existing class-counter archetype pairings.
- Race/ancestry/type/affinity benchmark creatures must challenge the relevant traits or mechanics, not simply copy a creature racial-type label.
- A creature may counter multiple dimensions, but each pairing must explain its actual counter expression and limitations.
- Creature racial type (document 22) and Envoy race/ancestry (this document) are distinct.
- A counter creature must have a species/individual identity, anatomy, natural weapons/armor, senses, movement, AI, tactics, techniques, weaknesses, lifecycle, and drops as applicable.
- Counter relationships do not grant automatic immunity, guaranteed detection, damage bonuses, or hard locks.
- Drops, resource-cell spawns, manual collection, and special containers remain separate resolution events.

## 9. Explicit non-decisions

This register does not:
- declare all listed source lineages to be final AO races;
- decide that broad research groupings are shared ancestry;
- assign a race-to-affinity mapping;
- grant every Envoy all affinities;
- define numerical racial bonuses or attribute allocations;
- convert the ten creature racial types into Envoy races;
- promote the proposed elemental matchup chart to canon;
- create missing composite affinities;
- assign divine status from appearance, class, or Tier;
- treat scale as a character's physical size or Tier;
- merge the lineage roster into a single generic fantasy ancestry tree.

The objective is complete, traceable Tier 9 coverage without inventing missing canon.
