## Influence is a causal bias, not guaranteed reality

World Influence distinguishes individual, household, organization, settlement, city, nation, region and world scopes. Gravity is one family of outcomes concerning attraction, repulsion, reach and retention; it is not permission to reinterpret every influence value as literal Newtonian force or make every desired event inevitable. Contributions need committed causes, scope and provenance rather than a self-declared importance score.

The implementation separates opportunity frequency, probability of an opportunity succeeding, quantity actually transferable, information delay and pressure retention. F56 accumulates fractional opportunity carry over time; changing time step cannot create extra trials. F26 changes a defined base probability within its bounds, not quantity or guaranteed arrival. F57 caps a successful event by stock, transport, demand and available population. These independent stages prevent one favorable influence number from multiplying every axis of the event.

## Propagation and retained causes

F58 requires distance, route speed and relay delay. A distant NPC cannot know a rumor before it arrives, even if the underlying Kernel records its source immediately. F59 decays prior pressure and adds unique causal contributions, with finite bounds. Shared household/organization membership alone does not broadcast private knowledge or allow the same cause to be counted at every scope as fresh pressure.

A successful migrant or trade opportunity transfers existing people or stock. It must debit the origin and credit the destination without duplicating population, wealth or supplies. The local fixture separates event frequency from success quantity; it is not a calibrated demographic or market model. A blocked route, absent stock or zero legal demand can keep the actual outcome at zero despite favorable probability.

## Offline activity and anti-loop policy

NPC work, law, information and trade continue under time-accounted schedules when Sovereigns are offline. Logging in or re-querying an event cannot reset its seed, create new time or redraw a failed opportunity. Stable event/cause IDs bind the opportunity and evidence. Coarse and realized population states must share the same reserves, not keep two copies of the source resources.
