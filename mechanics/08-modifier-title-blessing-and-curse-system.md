## Modifier identity and eligibility

A modifier is a versioned, attributable rule with a source, affected quantity, explicit scope, conditions, stacking group, duration and bounds. Titles, blessings, curses, gems, equipment, property and membership can supply different sources; their labels do not themselves grant a numerical bonus or permission. History predicates evaluate committed evidence with typed comparisons and explicit UNKNOWN handling. A rumor about an achievement is not proof that its grant conditions were met.

Eligibility for a title and possession of an authorized title grant are separate. Qualifying history is necessary where specified, but an eligible actor cannot self-issue an institutional or conceptual reward. The granting authority and permitted scope must be verified. Conversely, temporary suppression should not erase the historical fact that a title was legitimately granted. Revocation and renewed eligibility require their own recorded causes.

## Numerical application and conflicts

F14 applies declared flat adjustments before multiplying nonnegative factors, then clamps to the affected domain's explicit bounds. Multiple effects must first pass source, scope, stacking and conflict checks; F14 is arithmetic, not a universal decision to stack everything. A negative flat adjustment can lower a score while a relative factor remains nonnegative. Injury or curse conditions can also prohibit an action outright, rather than being represented as an arbitrary negative attack number.

For example, the existing scenario starts at100, adds20 and subtracts30, then multiplies by3/2 and1/2, yielding135/2 before any tighter domain cap. That does not allow bypassing missing affinity access, required prior techniques, physical reach or Heirloom custody. Neither temporary attributes nor a current effective skill bonus satisfies a permanent Tier or learned-mastery predicate.

## Evidence and anti-farming

F33 permits only approved typed fields from committed evidence and deduplicates by an explicitly chosen event/challenge/causal/context key. Replaying one rescue or renaming an identical challenge must not create five qualifying achievements. Ambiguous/unknown evidence blocks a grant rather than becoming an empty, trivially true predicate. Organizational membership conditions are evaluated at the relevant action time; expulsion can remove future benefits without deleting past grants or rewriting history.
