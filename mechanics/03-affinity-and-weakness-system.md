## Distinct states

Ownership records that an affinity was acquired. Access records whether a specific actor can presently draw on it. Active drawing/attunement records which channels are engaged in this action. Knowledge, technique acquisition, technique mastery and permission are further distinct conditions. Owning multiple affinities neither reveals every technique nor enables simultaneous use beyond F09. Each accessible affinity has its own EA reserve (738f §9); AP remains separate.

The creator direction distinguishes an acquired affinity's weakness from defensive benefits gained through the affinity currently drawn upon. A future signed profile must therefore contain both the owned-affinity weakness facts and action-specific active defense rules. The current fixtures validate ownership/access/channel distinctions but do NOT implement a canonical weakness graph. Missing edges or immunity rules cannot be inferred from elemental intuition, names, symmetry or an assistant's examples.

## Multi-affinity and progression handling

A technique's explicit profile identifies every required affinity and per-domain cost. All must be accessible and active within the channel limit. F71 counts distinct canonical acquired technique families whose actual profile contains a qualifying affinity. An alias cannot count twice. Under the proposed policy a multi-affinity family may count once toward each matching affinity's quota, but no mismatching family fills a quota. The policy is itself a proposal; unknown family maps or thresholds block Tier qualification.

No named affinity roster is manufactured to fill the requested catalogue. Once the exact roster and directed relationships are supplied, each record still needs provenance, effect scope, active-defense semantics, compatible technique mappings and negative tests before canonical acceptance can change.
