## Property, institutions and permissions

Property is a legal relationship backed by grants and transactions, not merely an object's physical parent. Possession, title, location, institutional membership, reputation and public knowledge are independently recorded. A holder may lack legal ownership; a legal owner may lack physical access. Joining an organization does not grant every asset, disclose every conversation or make a conceptual technique equivalent to institutional authority.

F65 proposes explicit institution stages: valid charter and sufficient members permit formation; actual assets, stock and treasury permit operation; a jurisdictional attestation is additionally needed for recognition. These thresholds are fixture proposals, not universal AO law. Development and privileges must name a granting authority, scope, conditions and expiry/revocation. Expulsion removes applicable future benefits; it does not erase history or silently confiscate every privately owned item.

## Conserving economic transactions

Income, upkeep, treasury and debt use abstract in-world currency subunits, never a real payment rail. F25 may produce a signed balance for accounting; it is not permission to spend nonexistent funds. Transfers reconcile counterparties, escrow or debt explicitly. F54 aggregates taxable income over the defined period before applying brackets so splitting one payment into many small transfers cannot evade the same total tax. F55 calculates only an explicitly agreed simple rate over an explicit period, not an unstated compounding or collection authority.

A production or trade success is bounded by actual stock, transport and demand through F57. Ownership transfer does not duplicate an asset across organization and household books. Work-in-progress, reserves and scrap need one accountable location; online/offline realization cannot count them twice. A facility's replenishment bonus must be serviced and fueled, not merely claimed by a building title.

## Law, belief and reputation

A witnessed allegation is a belief record with provenance, not omniscient legal truth. A judgment requires the defined jurisdiction/evidence/permission contract, while information reaches participants through actual routes and delay. Reputation can influence future probabilities or eligibility without mechanically coercing every NPC or changing a past event. National, city, organizational and household scopes remain distinct; benefits need explicit stacking conditions rather than being multiplied by every affiliation automatically.
